FAQs

Questions, answered clearly.

Here are answers to a few common questions. Contact us for guidance based on your specific situation.

Do I need GST registration for my business?

It depends on factors such as turnover, the type of supply, location and applicable GST rules. We can review your business details and help you understand the requirements.

Can you manage our accounts every month?

Monthly support may include bookkeeping, bank reconciliation, applicable GST/TDS filing coordination and management reports, based on the agreed scope.

Which business structure is right for a startup?

The right structure depends on ownership, liability, investment plans, compliance obligations and future goals. We can explain the common options so you can make an informed decision.

Can you help with GST or Income Tax notices?

We can help review the notice, identify information required and coordinate a response or refer the matter to an appropriately qualified professional where needed.

How are service fees decided?

Fees depend on the type of service, transaction volume, complexity, deadlines and ongoing support required. We share the proposed scope and fee before starting work.

How do I get started?

Send your details through the enquiry form or contact us by phone or WhatsApp. Please do not send passwords, OTPs or sensitive financial information through the public form.
